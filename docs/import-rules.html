MV Player import rules

How a track in the music folders comes to have a music video, to wait for your verdict on one, or to have none. One decision tree per stage of a lookup. This page is the reference, and a rule changes here first. A change is proposed in a copy of this page, additions in green and removals in red; once it is approved the marks come off, it becomes this page, and the code in src/core follows. Where the two disagree, that is a bug in one of them.

Proposed changes to the rules: additions and removals. Not approved yet; the code has not changed.

A Is the track looked up at all?

Asked of every file found in the music folders, and again when a lookup starts.

LibraryScanner::scan · AudioPrint::sameRecording · Matcher::isNonMvTrack · Matcher::isTalkTitle · TalkCheck::measure
  • A file in the music folders
    • A1It sounds like a recording already knownone more file of that recording Fingerprints differ in under 12% of their bits and the lengths are within 5 s. The files share one lookup and one video; the best of them (lossless, then bitrate, then the one known longest) is the one listened to and the one whose tags the video carries. A file at a new place with the same content keeps what was known about it.
    • A2Shorter than 1:20skipped A jingle, a skit, an interlude. A show's opening cut of a song ("TV size", about 1:30) stays in.
    • A3Its title marks an audio-only variantskipped instrumental, inst, off vocal, offvocal, karaoke, カラオケ, backing track. "TV size" is not one of them.
    • A4Its title says talk and it sounds like talkskipped Title: MC, talk, interview, commentary, voice drama and the like, as the whole title ("MC06", "Talk 2") or a tag at its end ("… (MC)"). Sound: at least 40% of moments far quieter than its average, and a pulse of at most 0.26. Either alone is not enough.
    • A5Otherwisesearched for: B

B Search

Four queries, tried in order, ten results each: artist title, then with MV, music video, official video added. The artist is the first album artist. Results are remembered for a day.

Matcher::searchQueries · YtDlp::search · ImportManager::runJob
  • After each query, the results so far are judged on sight (C)
    • B1A result is usableno further query; shortlist: C9 Usable: not dropped on sight, and it looks official (C7). Results of a later query rank two places below where YouTube put them.
    • B2YouTube refuses the querypause: I5
    • B3All four asked, none could be answeredfailed: A7
    • B4All four asked, nothing usableno video: A6 "None of N search results looked like an official video."

On a re-import the track's own video is a candidate whether or not the search returns it (H4).

R Definitions the trees lean on

Two measures come from laying the track against an upload's audio. Fingerprints say how much of the two is recognisably the same song. The waveform says how much is demonstrably the same recording, as a share of the shorter of the two.

ImportManager.cpp: audioMatches · audioReplaceable · fitted · ownRecording · sameRecordingAs · libraryIsBetter

R0The song

The upload's soundtrack contains the track:

  • fingerprints match at least 50% of the video, and
  • at least 50% of the track, or 80% of the video and 45 s.

A short edit of a song does not take the video of the full version, only one of about its own length.

R1The track's own recording

What lets an upload be taken outright with the track's audio. All of:

  • the upload is on the artist's channel (R5);
  • it is not a cut: at least 90% of the track's length;
  • the song is not interrupted: at most 15 s of other audio inside it;
  • at least 72% the same waveform, and 60% of what the fingerprints matched; or it is placed by offset (R2);
  • and one of: placed by offset; the same version (R3) on a vouched channel or with an artist named in the title or channel; or 97% the same waveform.

Measured: another master of a recording shows 77% and more; another singer over the same backing 67%; a remix 59%; live takes and covers 25–51%; a version made over the original 90%.

R2Placed by offset

The same performance in another mix has the track's waveform only here and there. It is taken for the recording, and the track laid whole at one offset, when all of:

  • the channel is the artist's by its name;
  • fingerprints cover 80%, the waveform 20%;
  • the loudness of the two rises and falls together (correlation 0.5);
  • the same version (R3);
  • a stretch of matching waveform sits at the offset the loudness gives.

R3The same version

By what the two are called. Not the same version when:

  • a version word (live, remix, cover, acoustic …) is in the upload's title and not in the track's title or album, or the other way round;
  • one names a version the other does not: "(English Ver.)", "Rap version". "Full ver." does not count, and a version only the album is named after need not be in the upload's title;
  • the upload credits a singer the track does not have: feat., CV, Vo. A synthesised voice is known by both its names, and on the artist's own channel is not another version.

R4The same recording as another track's

What lets a track share a video with other tracks, or wait on one with them. Either:

  • it is the recording in the upload: R1 without the conditions on the channel and on cuts; or
  • laid against a track that has the video or waits on it, 90% the same waveform.

Measured track against track: one recording on two releases 95–100%; the same song in another language 63–88%; a live take against the studio one 68%; another singer 0%.

R5The artist's channel

  • By its name: a name of the artist and nothing else but official, channel, music, YouTube, VEVO, TV, records, 公式, チャンネル. A channel named twice over ("A / B") counts by either name. "Producer feat. Singer": the producer's channel.
  • By MusicBrainz: its artist record links to the channel. Asked once per artist; only a sure match counts.
  • By you: accepting a video in review vouches for its channel, for every artist of the tracks that waited on it.

The last two are "vouched": as good as the artist's name on the upload.

R6The library's audio is at stake

"Use my library's audio" is on, and the track is the better audio: its quality exceeds YouTube's by more than 15%.

  • A lossless track beats everything.
  • Otherwise bitrate × 1.5 for Opus, × 1.2 for AAC and Vorbis.
  • YouTube's audio the same way; 130 kbit/s Opus where unknown.

YouTube's audio is only ever a video's main audio when the track is the lesser of the two.

R7Titled after this track

Decides whose a video under review is when the waveform cannot. All of:

  • the upload's title carries this track's title, word for word, outside brackets;
  • it carries none of the titles of the tracks waiting on it;
  • none of those tracks is the upload's waveform (72%);
  • the upload is not marked as a version one of them is and this track is not;
  • this track's audio is at stake (R6).

I Requests and pauses

Afterwards one job tests the water; the rest follow when a request gets through as a guest (not from the cache, and not through the account). Where the bot check stands and there is no account whose cookies work, the pause says so, and replacing the cookies resumes it.
